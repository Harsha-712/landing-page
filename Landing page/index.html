✺ About

What is DreamAI?

A living experiment at the intersection of imagination, artificial intelligence, and the ancient human need to dream.

"Every dream is a universe waiting to be born. DreamAI gives that universe a voice, a colour, and a cosmos."
— The DreamAI Manifesto
🧠
AI Dream Engine
Powered by Claude AI, our dream engine reads your imagination and generates rich, literary dream universes. Not just text — entire worlds with atmosphere, narrative, and soul. Each generation is unique and unrepeatable.
🎨
Living Themes
DreamAI doesn't just write about your dream — it becomes it. The entire visual environment shifts with each generation: starfields recolour, nebulae drift into new shapes, and the cosmos rearranges itself around your vision.
🌌
Theme Intelligence
Using a secondary AI classification layer, DreamAI detects the emotional and visual essence of your dream — cosmic, oceanic, ancient, cyberpunk — and maps it to a corresponding universe in real time.
⚡
Built for Speed
Both the dream generation and theme classification run in parallel, so your universe begins transforming before the story is even finished. Responsive, fluid, and crafted for a seamless experience on any device.
🔒
Private by Design
Your dreams stay yours. DreamAI does not store, share, or train on your inputs. Every session is ephemeral — like a dream itself. Once the page closes, the universe dissolves back into the void.
🌍
Open Imagination
DreamAI is built for everyone — writers seeking inspiration, artists looking for visual concepts, or anyone who has ever woken from a dream and wished they could return. No rules. No limits. Just imagination.
